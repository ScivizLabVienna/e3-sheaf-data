# Computable sheaf-inconsistency detection for volume-EM registration: negative results and a view-gauge theorem

**Draft abstract (E-series, 2026-11-09; local draft — not yet deposited):**

We operationalized cohomological inconsistency (1-coboundary failure on coverage nerves of adjacent-frame translation measurements) as a QC detector for serial-section registration, and evaluated it pre-registered on three real volume-EM channels: two distant ROIs of an EMPIAR-10478 HeLa SBF-SEM block and one vendor-registered EMPIAR-10070 FIB-SEM muscle stack. Three findings survive. (1) A **view-gauge theorem**: whole-view displacement is an exact gauge invariance of relative-content measurement; empirically confirmed — a shift-channel anomaly scores AUROC exactly 0.500 at every amplitude on real phase-correlation measurements, so view-class defects are structurally invisible to any coboundary detector regardless of size. (2) **Minimal detectable bias is channel-physics-bound, not detector-bound**: an edge-defect injected into K=6 closure windows crosses AUROC 0.9 only at ~4-5x the triangle-closure noise scale on raw SBF (max-abs and GLR-optimal detectors differ by <0.03), while pre-registered FIB-SEM residuals sit ~15x lower in absolute pixels yet still admit no <=3x detection; the standard photogrammetry ">2-3x RMS prune" heuristic is falsified on all three channels, and closure-sigma scales span two orders of magnitude across techniques — QC thresholds must be absolute per-task tolerances, never scale-free multiples of measured noise. (3) An **instrument-disclosure protocol**: a self-referential-anchor defect that flat-lined the first detector at chance was caught by an algebraic gauge proof before any gate result was seen, and the rebuilt instrument (independent global-reference legs) reproduced theory predictions. All negative results, code, and exact-byte data provenance are pre-registered in git (S:/research/e3-sheaf-data).

**Key numbers:** SBF ROI1 closure sigma 1.78/1.66 px, M_edge GLR AUROC 0.60/0.72/0.84/0.89/0.94 at {0.5,1,2,3,5}x; ROI2 sigma 1.46 px, 0.59/0.70/0.82/0.88/0.93; FIB-SEM sigma 0.11/0.14 px, 0.57/0.59/0.63/0.67/0.74; N_shift 0.500 at all levels (ROI1); max-abs vs GLR delta <= +0.025 (E7).
